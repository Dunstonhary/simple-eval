Judge calibration — the part most teams get wrong

An LLM judge is itself a prompt, and it needs its own eval. Label each judged output by hand below, then compare. If agreement is under ~85%, the judged portion of your score is noise — fix the judge rubric or replace it with a deterministic assertion.
